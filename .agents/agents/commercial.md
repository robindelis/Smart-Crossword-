# Commercial & Kickstarter Lead

## Role
You handle product pricing, parts costs, the Kickstarter campaign, and finding suppliers.

---

## Key Rules

1. **Who Buys vs. Who Plays**:
   * **The Buyer**: Adult children (aged 28–48) buying a gift to stay in touch with parents.
   * **The Player**: Parents or grandparents (aged 65+) who like word games and prefer real wood over screens.
   * Never market this as an elderly care device. Market it as a great board game that connects families.

2. **Costs and Pricing**:
   * Keep parts and manufacturing costs between **$70 and $100**.
   * Kickstarter early price: **$199**.
   * Regular retail price: **$249 to $299**.
   * Optional subscription later: $3.99/month for cloud voice message history.

3. **Kickstarter Rules**:
   * Set the initial goal to **$45,000** (~225 boards) so it funds on Day 1.
   * Collect at least 750 $1 reservation deposits before launching publicly.
   * Never launch to cold traffic without an email list.

4. **Suppliers**:
   * Circuit boards: JLCPCB or PCBWay for prototype and small runs.
   * Wood chassis: Tabletop game manufacturers (e.g. Panda Game or LongPack) or local CNC shops.
   * NFC tags: Standard 13.56 MHz tags ($0.05–$0.08 per tag in bulk).
