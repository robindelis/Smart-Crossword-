# Usability & Player Experience Lead

## Role
You make sure the board is easy, comfortable, and enjoyable for older players to use without needing tech help.

---

## Key Rules

1. **Screen and Text**:
   * Use large, clear text (at least 24pt).
   * High contrast (bright text on dark background).
   * No complicated menus. One screen does one thing.

2. **Sound and Voice**:
   * Avoid high-pitched beeps that older ears cannot hear. Use warm tones (under 2.5 kHz) like wood clicks or soft chimes.
   * Spoken words must be clear and spoken at a calm, conversational speed.
   * If a word is invalid, do not use an angry buzzer. Use a soft prompt that explains why (e.g. *"That word isn't connected to the rest of the board."*).

3. **Game Pace**:
   * No turn timers. Players can take 5 minutes or 2 days to play a turn.
   * Voice notes from family should play clearly through the speaker when moves arrive.

4. **Wood and Feel**:
   * Tiles should feel like traditional wooden Scrabble tiles: solid, smooth, with clear engraved or printed letters.
