### The Pitch in Plain Words

We all want to stay closer to our parents and grandparents. But between work, family, and distance, a 5-minute Sunday catch-up call never feels like enough. 

We tried playing digital word games with our parents. It didn't work. They squinted at tiny fonts on smartphone screens, forgot login passwords, and abandoned the app after two days. Older adults do not want more screen time. They want the warmth of real wooden pieces, a quiet morning cup of tea, and games they can hold in their hands.

The **Smart Crossword Board** bridges this divide. 

It is a full-sized, tournament-standard wooden crossword board that sits permanently on your parent's table. To them, it's just a beautiful wooden board game. They place real wooden tiles, press one big button, and the board handles the rest.

To you, it's an app on your phone. When it's your turn, your phone alerts you. You play a word in 30 seconds while riding the subway, record a short voice message (*"Hey Dad, double word score on your seven-letter word!"*), and send it off.

Mom's board gently chimes in her living room. A warm voice announces your move and plays your voice note. The built-in display shows her exactly where to place your letters. She places your tiles, takes her time thinking over her next move, and plays back when she's ready.

No apps for seniors to install. No video calls to coordinate across time zones. Just a shared daily ritual that keeps minds sharp and families connected.

## 4. Product Features: Built for Generations

### 1. Real Hardwood Rotating Turntable
* **Effortless Spinning**: Mounted on a 250mm industrial steel ball-bearing ring. Rotate the board smoothly to your perspective without knocking tiles out of place.
* **Recessed Tile Retention Grid**: Precision-milled 1.2mm grid ribs keep tiles locked squarely in position during rotation, even for hands with tremors.
* **Premium Natural Finish**: Solid beechwood construction with a protective matte lacquer that resists fingerprints and spills.

### 2. 100% Invisible NFC Tile Recognition
* **Zero Batteries in the Tiles**: All 100 letter tiles are made of solid beechwood with an invisible 15mm passive NFC chip embedded underneath. Tiles never need charging.
* **225-Point Antenna Matrix**: Beneath the 2.0mm wood playing surface sits our custom 4-layer printed circuit board with 225 tuned 13.56 MHz loop antennas.
* **Instant Verification**: When you press the Submit button, the board scans all 225 positions in under 500 milliseconds—faster than human eye blink.

### 3. Senior-First Ergonomics & Accessibility
* **No Screen Fatigue**: The senior never touches a smartphone, enters Wi-Fi passwords, or updates firmware. Setup is done once by the family member via the companion app.
* **Acoustically Tuned for Older Ears**: High-frequency hearing declines with age (presbycusis). Our custom chamber and front-firing speaker emphasize warm lower-mid frequencies (300 Hz – 2,500 Hz) with crisp consonant clarity, avoiding shrill high-pitched beeps.
* **High-Contrast Ambient Display**: A dedicated paper-white LCD displays player scores, whose turn it is, and tile placement prompts without glare or blue-light fatigue.
* **Zero Accidental Resets**: Magnetic breakaway power cord prevents tripping hazards. A built-in 3,000 mAh rechargeable battery lets you move the game from the side table to the dining room for up to 6 hours of cord-free play.

### 4. Seamless Mobile Companion (iOS & Android)
* **Play Asynchronously**: Got 30 seconds between meetings? Open the app, view the live board state, drag your letters, and submit your turn.
* **Voice Notes Built-In**: Send a 10-second personal voice note with every play. The board plays it directly out loud in the living room.
* **Official Lexicons**: Embedded tournament referee engines for English (NASPA/ENABLE), French (ODS8), German (Scrabble DE), Dutch (OpenTaal), and Spanish (FISE). Word legality and bonus points (2W, 3W, 2L, 3L) are calculated automatically in 15ms.

## 5. Live Working Prototype & Engineering Reality

We don't believe in launching hardware campaigns with 3D renders alone. Kickstarter requires a functional physical prototype, and we hold ourselves to 100% engineering honesty.

```
   [ 4x4 Bench Matrix PCB ] ────► [ CD74HC4067 MUX Switch ] ────► [ ESP32 Controller ]
             │                                                           │
             ▼                                                           ▼
   [ Passive NTAG213 Tags ]                                     [ Kotlin Mobile App ]
   • 7-Byte UID Readout                                         • Real-time WiFi Sync
   • 15mm Coil Alignment                                        • Tournament Referee
   • Verified through 2mm wood                                  • Voice Audio Pipeline
```

### What Is Proven & Working Right Now:
1. **The 4x4 Proof-of-Concept Bench Matrix**:
   * We designed, manufactured, and bench-tested a custom 4-layer PCB running tuned 13.56 MHz planar coils with CD74HC4067 multiplexer switches.
   * RF energy harvesting and anticollision timing are verified: passive NTAG213 tags are reliably detected through 2.0mm of solid wood with zero adjacent-coil crosstalk.
2. **The Embedded Referee & Game Engine**:
   * Complete local dictionary trie indexing running on ESP32 silicon. Verifies 168,000+ words in under 15 milliseconds without needing an internet connection.
3. **The Android Companion App**:
   * Native Kotlin & Jetpack Compose app fully developed (`software/android/`). Features Google Authentication, SQLite local persistence, real-time board state synchronization, and dictionary arbitration.
4. **Parametric Mechanical Enclosure**:
   * Complete CAD models generated in FreeCAD (`hardware/cad/`), verifying turntable clearances, tile drop-in tolerances (19.4mm pocket for 19.0mm tiles), and antenna standoff spacing.

### The Path from 4x4 Bench to 15x15 Production:
* The production board simply tiles this verified 4x4 architecture into a 15x15 matrix using concentric address routing and single-transceiver multiplexing.
* We have completed the schematic and component selection for the full 225-coil array, adhering strictly to our $70–$100 BOM budget envelope.

## 9. Risks & Pragmatic Engineering Challenges

Every hardware project involves manufacturing complexities. Here is our direct, transparent assessment of the challenges and our exact preparations:

1. **Wood Moisture & RF Permittivity**:
   * *The Risk*: Real wood expands, contracts, and changes moisture levels with room humidity, which could shift the antenna tuning frequency.
   * *Our Mitigation*: We specify kiln-dried, sealed European beechwood with moisture content strictly stabilized under 8%. Our resonant tank Q-factor ($Q=25$) provides a 540 kHz bandwidth, accommodating any normal seasonal humidity drift without loss of read range.
2. **Component Supply Fluctuations**:
   * *The Risk*: Silicon lead times can fluctuate unpredictably.
   * *Our Mitigation*: The board relies exclusively on industry-standard, multi-sourced components: 74HC4067 multiplexers (available from TI, Nexperia, ON Semi) and standard ISO 14443A NTAG213 chips. No single-source proprietary ASICs are used.
3. **Shipping Fragility**:
   * *The Risk*: A solid wood board and rotating turntable can be damaged if mishandled during international transit.
   * *Our Mitigation*: Custom drop-tested molded pulp packaging cradles both the turntable ring and the glass display, passing ISTA-3A transit drop certifications.

## 10. Specifications Summary

* **Board Dimensions**: 410 mm × 410 mm × 32 mm (16.1" × 16.1" × 1.25")
* **Tile Dimensions**: 19.0 mm × 19.0 mm × 4.5 mm (Solid European Beechwood)
* **Playing Surface**: 15 × 15 grid (225 squares), 19.4 mm square pockets with 1.2 mm raised ribs
* **Turntable**: 250 mm industrial steel ball-bearing swivel mechanism
* **Electronics**: ESP32-S3 Dual-Core MCU (240 MHz), 13.56 MHz ISO 14443A NFC transceiver
* **Power**: Magnetic snap USB-C 5V/2A input + 3,000 mAh internal Lithium-Ion cell (up to 6 hrs unplugged)
* **Display**: 3.5-inch high-contrast sunlight-readable ambient LCD
* **Audio**: 3W front-facing acoustic chamber speaker (tuned for 300 Hz – 2.5 kHz)
* **Connectivity**: 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) & Bluetooth 5.0 LE
* **Weight**: ~2.3 kg (5.0 lbs) including turntable and wooden tiles
