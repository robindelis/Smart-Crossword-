# Kickstarter Video Script: Smart Crossword Board
**Length**: 2 Minutes, 30 Seconds  
**Tone**: Warm, Cinematic, Emotional, Honest Craftsmanship  
**Music**: Starts with an intimate, acoustic piano/guitar melody, building into an uplifting, rhythmic modern orchestral track.

---

## Act 1: The Disconnect (0:00 – 0:35)

| Time | Visual / Video Action | Audio / Voiceover |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:08** | **Wide Shot**: Morning sun streaming through a cozy living room window. An 76-year-old grandmother sits at her dining table with a cup of tea. In front of her is an old, dusty Scrabble box. She turns a wooden letter tile in her fingers. | **Warm Narration (Narrator)**:<br>*"We all want to stay connected to the people who raised us. But between work, busy schedules, and living cities apart... life moves fast."* |
| **0:08 - 0:18** | **Quick Cut**: 32-year-old adult child (Sarah) walking down a busy city street, glancing at her smartphone. Notifications pile up: Slack, work emails, calendar invites. She looks stressed. | *"A quick Sunday phone call never feels like enough. And while we live on screens, our parents and grandparents cherish the tactile world—the physical things they can hold, touch, and savor."* |
| **0:18 - 0:30** | **Close-up**: Grandmother looking at an iPad with tiny icons, squinting, trying to read a crossword app, looking frustrated, and setting the tablet down. | *"Asking them to squint at tiny apps and remember passwords steals the joy of playing together."* |
| **0:30 - 0:35** | **Fade to Black**. Music pauses for a heartbeat. | *"What if you could play your favorite board game together every day... without them ever touching a screen?"* |

## Act 2: The Magic Reveal & Live Play (0:35 – 1:25)

| Time | Visual / Video Action | Audio / Voiceover |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:35 - 0:45** | **Cinematic Beauty Shot**: Smooth camera glide across a handcrafted wooden turntable crossword board on a coffee table. The wood is rich walnut with precision raised grid ribs. A subtle, discrete high-contrast screen sits on the bezel. | **Music swells: Uplifting acoustic track.**<br>*"Introducing the Smart Crossword Board. The world’s first connected, luxury crossword board designed for real wooden play across any distance."* |
| **0:45 - 0:55** | **Split Screen**: Sarah on the subway train opens the companion smartphone app. She drags virtual tiles to spell **"B-R-E-E-Z-E"** and taps a microphone icon, speaking: *"Hi Grandma, got you on a double-word!"* She taps Submit. | **Sarah (Voice)**:<br>*"Hi Grandma, got you on a double-word!"* |
| **0:55 - 1:10** | **Grandmother's House**: The wooden board softly chimes with a warm, low-frequency tone. A gentle voice announces: *"Sarah played 'BREEZE' for 28 points!"* Followed by Sarah’s recorded voice note. | **Board Voice + Audio Chime**.<br>*"Grandma doesn’t need a smartphone. The board's high-contrast display diagrams exactly where to place the tiles."* |
| **1:10 - 1:25** | **Close-up**: Grandmother smiles, reaches into the velvet pouch, picks out B-R-E-E-Z-E, and places them onto the highlighted grid squares. The board gives a satisfying confirmation chime. Then she looks at her rack, chuckles, and places **"P-E-A-C-H"** intersecting the 'E'. She presses the solid brass **Submit** button. | *"She places her physical wooden tiles and hits Submit. The board automatically checks the official tournament dictionary, tallies the score, and beams the move straight to Sarah's phone."* |
