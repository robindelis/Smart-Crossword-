# Kickstarter Video Script: Smart Crossword Board
**Length**: 2 Minutes, 30 Seconds  
**Tone**: Warm, clear, direct  
**Music**: Starts with simple acoustic guitar, builds into an upbeat melody.

---

## Part 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:35)

| Time | What You See | What You Hear |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:08** | A grandmother sits at her kitchen table with tea. She turns an old wooden Scrabble tile in her hand. | **Narrator**:<br>*"We all want to stay in touch with our parents and grandparents. But life gets busy, and living far apart makes it harder."* |
| **0:08 - 0:18** | Quick cut to a daughter (Sarah) on a busy city street looking at her phone. Work notifications pop up. | *"A quick Sunday phone call never feels like enough. But while we live on our phones, our parents prefer real, physical things they can hold."* |
| **0:18 - 0:30** | The grandmother squints at a tablet screen with tiny text, gets frustrated, and puts it down. | *"Asking them to squint at tiny apps and deal with passwords takes the fun out of playing together."* |
| **0:30 - 0:35** | Screen fades black. | *"What if you could play a real board game together every day, from anywhere in the world, without them touching a phone?"* |

---

## Part 2: How It Works (0:35 – 1:25)

| Time | What You See | What You Hear |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:35 - 0:45** | Camera pans over the wooden board on a table. Clean walnut finish with raised grid lines. A bright screen sits on the frame. | *"This is the Smart Crossword Board. A real wooden board game that connects directly to your phone."* |
| **0:45 - 0:55** | Split screen: Sarah on the subway opens the phone app, drags tiles to spell **"B-R-E-E-Z-E"**, records a 3-second voice note, and hits send. | **Sarah (Voice Note)**:<br>*"Hey Grandma, got you on a double word!"* |
| **0:55 - 1:10** | At the grandmother's house: the board chimes. A clear voice announces: *"Sarah played BREEZE for 28 points!"* and plays Sarah's voice note. The screen shows where to place the letters. | *"Grandma doesn't need a phone. The board announces the move out loud and shows the letters on the screen."* |
| **1:10 - 1:25** | Grandma places the tiles on the board. The board chimes in confirmation. She then plays her own word, **"P-E-A-C-H"**, and hits the big button. | *"She puts down her wooden tiles and presses Submit. The board checks the official dictionary, calculates the score automatically, and sends the move straight to Sarah's phone."* |

---

## Part 3: The Engineering (1:25 – 1:55)

| Time | What You See | What You Hear |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1:25 - 1:40** | The founder in a workshop. A 3D model shows the wooden top lifting off to reveal the circuit board underneath with 225 sensor coils. | **Founder**:<br>*"Under the wood is an invisible sensor grid. It reads all 100 letter tiles in less than half a second through solid wood using radio tags."* |
| **1:40 - 1:55** | Demonstrating the turntable spinning smoothly on ball bearings, the magnetic power plug, and moving it to the dining table on battery power. | *"The whole board spins smoothly on a turntable. It has a high-contrast screen made for older eyes, a clear speaker, and a built-in battery so you can play anywhere in the house."* |

---

## Part 4: The Ask (1:55 – 2:30)

| Time | What You See | What You Hear |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1:55 - 2:10** | Quick montage of different families playing: a dad and son, a student and grandfather laughing over a high-scoring word. | *"It turns quick texts into a fun daily habit that keeps family close and keeps older minds active."* |
| **2:10 - 2:25** | The board on a table next to a phone. Text on screen shows Early Bird price: $199 (40% off retail). | *"We've built the prototypes and lined up our manufacturing partners. Now we need your help to fund the first production run."* |
| **2:25 - 2:30** | Kickstarter logo and link. Grandma's voice over black: *"Your turn, Sarah."* | *"Back the Smart Crossword Board on Kickstarter today."* |
