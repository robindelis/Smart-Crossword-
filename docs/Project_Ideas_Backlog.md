# Project Ideas & Feature Backlog

This document captures electromechanical, UX, and product ideas to consider as the prototype advances.

---

## 1. Hardware & Mechanical Ideas

* **360° Magnetic Turntable Power Ring**:
  * Use concentric circular copper slip-ring traces with sprung pogo pins in the turntable center.
  * Lets the board spin infinitely in both directions while staying continuously powered without cord tangling.
* **Acoustic Speaker Chamber**:
  * Molded internal chamber for the 40mm speaker with lower-mid bass porting (300 Hz – 2.5 kHz).
  * Makes voice announcements sound warm, rich, and clear for seniors with age-related high-frequency hearing loss.
* **Magnetic Tile Alignment Ribs**:
  * CNC milled wood grid with micro-magnets or precision 0.8mm raised wood ridges to keep tiles snapped straight into square centers.
* **Travel Locking Lid**:
  * A clear acrylic or wood-veneered clip-on cover with foam backing that holds tiles tightly in place so an in-progress game can be moved to another table without disturbing the board.

---

## 2. Emotional UX & Gameplay Ideas

* **Grandchild Voice-Note Transmission**:
  * When family members play a turn on their smartphone, they can record a 5-second voice message ("Love you Grandma, that was a tough word!").
  * The board speaker plays the message out loud right after announcing the move.
* **Interactive Blank Tile Selector**:
  * When an unassigned blank tile is detected on the board, the console screen displays a large, high-contrast alphabet selector so the senior can tap their letter choice.
* **Casual vs. Tournament Dictionary Mode**:
  * A setting allowing families to switch between strict official Scrabble tournament rules and a relaxed dictionary that accepts informal words.
* **Turn Memory & Board History**:
  * An e-ink or LCD summary button to review the last 5 turns played, words formed, and point breakdowns.
